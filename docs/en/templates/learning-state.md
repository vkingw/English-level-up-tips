## Cross-session Recovery Prompt

```text
Below is my learning-state file. In no more than six bullets, restate my goal, current evidence, main errors, and next action. Flag conflicts or missing information and do not pretend to remember other chats. Design one exercise only for the “smallest next task”: let me answer first, then give feedback against the acceptance criteria. Do not provide a complete answer unless I ask. Finish with four paste-ready updates: completed, evidence, errors, next action.

[paste Learning State]
```

An AI summary is not the source of truth. Resume from the file and saved work.
