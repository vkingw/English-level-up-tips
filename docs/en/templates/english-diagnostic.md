Scoring reference: `0=not completed`, `1=partial or cue-dependent`, `2=stable under the stated conditions`. Record the reason for each score, not only the number.

Repeat under the same conditions or a close parallel task on day 30 and week 12. Keep the raw sample, assisted version, delayed performance, and scoring reasons instead of comparing only polished products. Put the next variable into the [90-Day Cycle Map](90-day-cycle.md).
